# Google Play listing — Nest Story

## Title

Nest Story

## Short description

A home inventory for rooms, items, prices, and photos.

## Full description

Nest Story keeps the things in the house in one place. Add a house, rooms, and areas. Save a photo, a purchase price, a warranty date, and a reminder to maintain or replace something.

Search by name, category, or room. See the total value of what you own. Export the list as CSV or PDF.

Eight items are free. Nest Story Pro keeps more, plus the shop’s looks. Remove ads and Pro are one-time unlocks. Coins come from saving an item, the daily reward, or 10 coin packs.

Remote config: https://api2.blwsmartware.net/N322.json — if disable=1, Play purchase sheets stay hidden. The shop still spends coins.
